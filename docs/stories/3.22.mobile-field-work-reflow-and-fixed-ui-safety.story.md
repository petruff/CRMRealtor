## Story

**As Judith, a realtor working from a phone and tablet throughout the day,**  
**I want** Activities and the shared Omnix shell to reflow without horizontal scrolling, undersized controls or fixed-interface collisions,  
**so that** I can review, select and complete follow-ups quickly in the field without losing any action, context or confidence.

**Traceability:** Wave 1, item 1 of `docs/audits/2026-08-20-independent-realtor-journey-ui-ux-audit.md#prioritized-roadmap`; blocking findings `#mobile-activities-breaks-reflow` and `#mobile-shell-and-omnix-launcher-compete-with-work`; Story 3.21 AC4, AC11 and AC12; Story 3.13 AC9.

**Dependencies:** Stories 3.1 and 3.21 supply the task/work-queue and current shared design-system contracts. Story 3.13 supplies Today-specific fixed-navigation and 44 px expectations. This story is a bounded source-remediation and regression story; it does not replace the incomplete manual certification matrices owned by Stories 3.13 and 3.21.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. At viewport sizes **390×844**, **768×1024** and **1440×900**, `/activities` has no page-level horizontal overflow: `document.documentElement.scrollWidth <= document.documentElement.clientWidth`. Task content, due date, state, selection and row actions remain readable and reachable without clipping. At compact and intermediate widths, the task collection uses an accessible reflowed presentation or a deliberately bounded, visibly discoverable and keyboard-operable local scroller; the existing full-width desktop table may remain where it fits.
2. The responsive task presentation preserves one canonical task collection and every existing capability: select one, select all visible, selected count, complete, reopen, archive with confirmation, bulk complete, bulk archive, Google Calendar preparation when authorized, status/due-time display in the workspace timezone, empty state and the Add follow-up form. A responsive implementation must not mount duplicate actionable controls or duplicate successful form submissions for the same task.
3. Every interactive control in the critical mobile Activities and shared-shell journey has an effective target of at least **44×44 CSS px**, including task and select-all checkboxes through their visible label/hit-area contract, task row actions, bulk actions, mobile-header actions, bottom navigation, assistant launcher and dialog close control. The native checkbox remains perceivable, associated with its accessible name and operable by touch, pointer and keyboard.
4. At narrow widths, the mobile header preserves the Omnix mark, product identity and current page label while exposing no more than two or three immediate utility actions. Remaining utilities move behind one clearly named **More** control or an equally bounded progressive-disclosure pattern. Search, Alerts, Settings, Workspaces, theme toggle and POST-only Sign out all remain discoverable and semantically correct; no route or account action is removed, renamed ambiguously or converted to an unsafe client-only mutation.
5. The compact utility disclosure is keyboard and screen-reader operable: its trigger exposes resolvable expanded/control state, focus enters a logical first action, `Escape` closes it, focus returns to the trigger, visible focus is never clipped, and route change closes transient shell UI. It introduces no focus trap, background business-data write, persisted preference or duplicated navigation authority.
6. On phone viewports, the closed Omnix launcher uses a compact, recognizable and at least 44×44 target whose resting visual footprint does not obscure task actions, page CTAs, bottom navigation or the mobile-header utilities. Its label may collapse visually while its accessible name remains `Open Omnix assistant`. Opening it preserves the existing modal, focus trap, Escape/close behavior, body-scroll containment, conversation scrolling, safe-area composer padding, route-close behavior and grounded assistant action.
7. Fixed-interface collision checks prove that the mobile header, bottom navigation, closed assistant launcher, open assistant dialog and primary page actions do not overlap in their relevant states at 390 and 768, including safe-area insets and native 200% zoom/reflow. Main content retains sufficient end padding to reach its final action without being hidden behind fixed navigation. The assistant remains absent on `/omnix` as it is today.
8. Activities populated, dense, empty, loading and error states remain truthful and usable in both light and dark themes. Loading exposes a named status and respects reduced motion; error states state what failed and what was not changed, preserve safe Retry/Today/Contacts destinations, and do not mutate tasks or filters. Dense fixtures include long titles/descriptions, multiple task states, Google Calendar actions and enough rows to exercise scrolling and bulk selection.
9. A keyboard-only critical path passes in both desktop and compact presentation: skip to main content; reach Activities filters; select a task; complete or archive it through the existing confirmation boundary; reach Add follow-up; open and close More; open and close Omnix; and reach the bottom navigation. Focus order follows reading order, focus remains visible, status messages use the existing live-region semantics, and no information or action depends only on color, hover or motion.
10. Before visual approval, a repeatable CLI/test workflow emits or saves a machine-readable responsive receipt for each required viewport/state with at least: route, viewport, theme, zoom mode, `clientWidth`, `scrollWidth`, measured interactive-target minima, fixed-element intersection result, keyboard result and console/hydration result. Expected error-boundary logging is identified separately from unexpected runtime errors. Receipts are indexed under `docs/qa/evidence/3.22/` and cannot be replaced by screenshots alone.
11. The remediation uses the current Next.js 15 App Router, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4, Lucide icons, CSS token/primitives and existing server actions. It adds no component framework, client state manager, schema migration, provider, route, task status, background job or analytics event. Task mutations retain their current server-action, authorization, confirmation, idempotency and Google approval boundaries.
12. Automated regressions cover: compact/intermediate Activities reflow; absence of page overflow; one actionable task representation; complete/reopen/archive/bulk selection preservation; 44 px effective targets; mobile utility disclosure semantics and focus return; compact launcher behavior; fixed-element collision geometry; `/omnix` launcher suppression; light/dark; reduced motion; and empty/loading/error/dense states. Tests must fail if `min-w-[38rem]` again creates page-level overflow or if a native task-selection hit area returns to the observed 13×13 effective target.
13. From `packages/crm`, focused tests, `npm run lint`, `npm run typecheck`, `npm test` and `npm run build` pass. Independent UX and QA reviews execute and inspect the 390/768/1440 light/dark matrix, native 200% zoom, keyboard-only path, reduced motion, fixed-element collisions and saved receipts. No production handoff occurs until both independent verdicts are PASS and the story file contains the exact tested file list and evidence index.
14. No capability regression is introduced in Today, Contacts, Activities, Pipeline, Alerts, Insights, Mailers, Connections, Data & API, Settings, Workspaces, theme persistence, authentication, sign-out or Omnix AI. The current design-token authority and `/welcome` isolation remain intact. Any defect discovered outside the named Activities/shell/launcher boundary is documented and routed to its owning story rather than silently expanded into this implementation.

### Responsive behavior contract

- One business action must have one actionable DOM authority. If a responsive technique renders separate table/card views, the inactive view must not remain focusable, successful, named for form submission or announced as duplicate content.
- A bounded local scroller is acceptable only when semantic reflow would destroy important row comparison. It must be visually discoverable, keyboard reachable, contained within the viewport and must not make the page itself wider.
- A 44×44 effective target may use a correctly associated label/wrapper around a native checkbox; the visible checkbox state and focus indication must remain perceivable.
- Fixed-element collision means non-zero geometric intersection between a resting fixed element and an actionable content region, or content that cannot be scrolled clear of the bottom navigation. The receipt must measure rather than infer this result from a screenshot.
- At native 200% zoom, horizontal scrolling of a deliberately bounded data region may remain only when it is the documented local-overflow fallback; page-level two-dimensional scrolling is a failure.
