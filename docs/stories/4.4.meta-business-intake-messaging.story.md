## Story

**As a** workspace owner,  
**I want** eligible Facebook/Instagram business-message enquiries ingested into Omnix with source context and review,  
**so that** social leads stop being retyped while unsupported personal access, duplicate contacts and silent automation remain impossible.

**Traceability:** RQ-02 and RQ-26; the latest Product Owner ruling confirms inbound social enquiries as the first Meta outcome. Lead Ads and outbound DM automation are not implicitly approved.

**Dependencies:** Story 3.5 supplies Business Login/OAuth boundaries, encrypted secrets, signed webhooks, jobs, receipts, approvals and disconnect. Story 1.3 supplies idempotent validated intake; Story 3.1 supplies incomplete-record review/activity evidence; Stories 3.0/3.1 are Done. Story 3.2 is used when available for canonical contact-point matching but is not the source of Meta sender identity.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **CLI-first provider/version/permission truth.** Before UI, CLI commands list the configured pinned Graph API version, exact requested/granted permissions, eligible/selected Page and Instagram Professional assets, connection health, webhook subscriptions, intake deliveries/review outcomes, reconciliation and disconnect. Production startup and authorization fail closed if version/permission lists are unpinned, empty, unapproved or incompatible. No story text guesses a permission name: T0 must record the official current set and App Review evidence before enablement.
2. **Owner-only Business Login and asset binding.** The owner initiates Meta Business Login on Meta's surface and explicitly selects eligible assets returned by the authorized account. Connection metadata binds workspace, Meta business/account and selected Page/Instagram asset IDs; tokens remain encrypted/server-only. Assistants cannot connect, change assets/scopes, read credentials or disconnect. Personal-account passwords, cookies, browser scraping and caller-supplied Graph endpoints/assets/workspaces are forbidden.
3. **Verified, replay-safe webhook ingress.** Verification challenge and POST signatures are handled through the pinned adapter and validated over bounded raw input before parsing/mutation. The opaque connection endpoint plus selected asset identifies the workspace; payload workspace/page claims do not. Provider event/delivery IDs and stable hashes deduplicate replays. Duplicate, wrong-asset, invalid, stale or out-of-order events cause no second intake/activity mutation and produce redacted evidence only.
4. **Provenance-preserving normalized enquiry.** Each accepted inbound business-message event records provider, selected asset, channel (`facebook | instagram`), opaque sender/conversation/message IDs, provider timestamp, correlation and a minimized encrypted content reference under the approved retention policy. Raw content, names, handles, signatures and tokens never enter operational logs/receipts. The normalized event retains enough authorized context for a human to understand the enquiry and open the provider conversation without pretending that Omnix knows unsupported identity facts.
5. **Conservative match/create/review.** Exact existing provider external identity links to the same contact. Otherwise, an exact email/phone explicitly supplied through an eligible event may use Story 1.3/3.2 conservative identity rules. No trustworthy contact point produces a Story 3.1 incomplete-record/review item with the social source, asset and encrypted message context; it does not fabricate email/phone or auto-merge by display name/handle. Multiple, archived or conflicting matches remain review-only. Human merge/create records actor, decision and preserved provenance idempotently.
6. **Durable normalization and reconciliation.** Verified deliveries enqueue idempotent Story 3.5 normalization jobs and acknowledge only after durable acceptance. Worker restart, duplicate webhook and review retries cannot create a second contact/activity. Missed-delivery reconciliation is bounded and uses only provider-supported streams/endpoints approved in T0; absence of a supported reconciliation API is shown honestly and compensated by health/last-event monitoring, never invented polling. Provider outages, token expiry and removed assets become degraded/reauthorization states.
7. **Human messaging boundary.** This story's required live outcome is inbound business-message intake and human review. No automated or AI-generated outbound DM is sent. A future human-authored reply may use Story 3.5 owner approval only after Product approves the exact reply JTBD, Meta approves the needed permission/assets, the applicable conversation window/escalation rules are versioned, and a separate acceptance update/story passes. Until then reply controls are absent or explicitly unavailable—not decorative.
8. **Retention, safety and access.** Approved workspace users can view only message context necessary for review/contact history; assistant access follows the versioned workspace policy. Retention/deletion/export and provider data-use terms are approved before real content persistence. Removing provider content under policy does not erase redacted provenance/activity/receipt history. Prompt/AI processing, lead scoring, Fair Housing inference and automated response from DM text are absent.
9. **Disconnect/revocation and retained CRM history.** Disconnect immediately blocks new jobs/webhook mutation, removes subscriptions where supported, revokes access, cryptoshreds local tokens and reports confirmed versus unconfirmed outcomes. Existing contact/external-link/review/activity provenance remains under policy. A later reconnect to different assets cannot silently attach old sender identities or consume old webhook deliveries.
10. **Truthful UX, tests and real-account UAT.** `/connections` displays exact business account/assets, permissions, version, webhook health, last accepted event/reconciliation, review backlog and `disabled | authorizing | active | degraded | app review blocked | reauthorization required | disconnected`. It never labels a fixture, personal account, Lead Ads or inbound-only connection as full outbound messaging. Tests cover two workspaces/roles, OAuth/account/asset swap, empty/unapproved permissions, webhook challenge/signature/replay/wrong asset, duplicate/out-of-order events, identity ambiguity, review idempotency, retention/redaction and disconnect. CLI smoke, database tests, desktop/390px accessibility, lint, typecheck, full tests and build pass. Real UAT proves connect/select assets → verified Facebook or Instagram enquiry → review/create/link with provenance → replay no-op → disconnect/revoke before a live claim.

## Out of Scope

- Personal-account access, password/cookie capture, browser scraping, unsupported Instagram accounts or unapproved assets/permissions.
- Lead Ads ingestion, ad management, marketing analytics, automated/AI replies, bulk outreach or promises of unrestricted DM history.
- Inferring identity/contact points from handles, treating a social message as consent for email/SMS, or presenting deterministic fixtures as Meta approval.
