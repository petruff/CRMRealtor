# Story 6.3 — Premium Contributor Explorer and Final Product Audit

## Status

In Review

## Story

As a realtor, I need large metric-contributor sets to remain understandable and actionable, and I need the production CRM audited end to end, so that I can trust the system without navigating an overwhelming record dump or discovering broken core workflows during daily use.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Exact Contributors preserves the existing exact, period-aware and evidence-aware contributor contract while replacing the unbounded list with a premium explorer suitable for hundreds of records.
2. The explorer provides an immediate metric summary, contributor count, clear scope and evidence state, search, entity-type filtering, useful deterministic ordering, bounded pages, and accessible previous/next navigation.
3. Contributor rows expose recognizable identity, human-readable context and a clear record action without internal IDs or implementation vocabulary.
4. Search, filters and pagination work without a full-page round trip; changing the selected metric or period keeps the canonical Insights URL behavior and resets local explorer state safely.
5. Empty, unavailable, incomplete and filtered-empty states are visually distinct, truthful and actionable.
6. The contributor experience has no horizontal overflow at 390, 768 or 1440 pixels, supports keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced motion and semantic announcements for result changes.
7. The live transaction flow is re-audited from authorization and exact-money persistence through pipeline synchronization, period aggregation, zero/error states and production rendering. No synthetic transaction is written to Judith's production workspace.
8. A final audit covers authenticated critical routes, primary create/edit/import/task/pipeline/Insights/Omnix/mailer/connection/workspace journeys, mobile and desktop layout, accessibility signals, browser errors, failed network requests, data truthfulness, authorization boundaries and connector health.
9. Confirmed in-scope defects found during the audit are fixed and regression-tested before release; external-provider, credential or user-authorization blockers remain explicit and are not presented as fixed.
10. Lint, typecheck, complete tests and production build pass. The story records changed files, release evidence, corrected defects and every known residual issue before closure.

## Evidence Rules

- Exactness is preserved: filtering and pagination change presentation, not metric membership.
- No successful empty state may be used to hide a failed or unavailable read.
- Provider connection labels must reflect stored health evidence and never infer success from an OAuth redirect alone.
- Production validation remains read-only unless a reversible test action is explicitly safe and required.
- An audit can reduce known risk but cannot prove the absolute absence of unknown defects.
