## Story

**As Judith,**  
**I want** Today, Alerts, contact follow-up and Activities to use the same automatic Hot/Warm/Nurture cadence,  
**so that** I receive a realistic, completable daily work list instead of hundreds of duplicate or artificially urgent items after an import.

**Traceability:** Judith confirmed Hot weekly, Warm weekly or every two weeks, Nurture monthly, automatic next follow-up with manual adjustment; production evidence in `docs/audits/2026-08-21-launch-readiness-realtor-operations-audit.md`; Stories 1.1, 1.2, 3.1, 3.16 and 3.13.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. The only default cadence is the client-approved rhythm: Hot 7 days, Warm 7 days initially with a documented manually adjustable 14-day option, and Nurture 30 days. The current unapproved automatic 8x8/weekly Nurture acceleration is removed from domain behavior and customer copy.
2. New real-time leads still appear immediately as first-contact work. Historical imports without a last-contact timestamp follow their classified cadence and are not all promoted to urgent solely because the CRM record was created during migration.
3. Today, Alerts, contact detail and Activities derive from one canonical, workspace-scoped follow-up occurrence per contact and due date. One underlying follow-up cannot appear as two independently completable tasks or produce contradictory counts.
4. Every due follow-up is visible and completable from Activities or the contact record. Completion records the touch, advances `new` to `contacted` when applicable, schedules the next cadence date and removes the resolved occurrence from Today/Alerts on the next authorized read.
5. Automatic materialization is idempotent and bounded. Refreshes, retries, concurrent reads, imports and scheduler runs cannot create duplicate open tasks. Manual tasks remain distinct and are never overwritten by cadence generation.
6. Imported contacts are repaired through a dry-run-first, versioned, reversible operation. It reports aggregate counts only, skips archived/dormant contacts and manual date overrides, preserves explicit last-contact facts and records immutable activity evidence.
7. The Judith workspace dry run proves the resulting distribution before apply: Hot/Warm work remains appropriately near-term; Nurture work is distributed by monthly cadence; no single migration timestamp creates a false all-database emergency. Exact counts are evidence, not hard-coded acceptance values.
8. Customer-facing wording says `First contact`, `Overdue`, `Due today`, `Coming up` and `Monthly follow-up`; it does not expose rule IDs, raw field keys, UUIDs, policy versions or 8x8 terminology.
9. Tests cover real-time new lead, same-day import, historical import, Hot/Warm/Nurture, manual 14-day Warm override, no-last-contact, explicit last-contact, dormant stages, manual next-date override, duplicate scheduler runs, completion/reschedule and Today/Alerts/Activities count parity.
10. Focused/full tests, lint, typecheck and build pass. Authenticated production evidence compares Today, Alerts, Activities and representative contacts before/after the dry run and apply; deployment remains blocked until QA approves the data mutation and rollback plan.

## Out of Scope

- Predictive lead scoring, autonomous outreach, new notification channels or provider sends.
- Changing Mailchimp/Google/Twilio/Meta authority, transaction/P&L domains or the approved Today visual composition.
- Hiding imported contacts, silently marking them contacted or inventing historical conversations.
