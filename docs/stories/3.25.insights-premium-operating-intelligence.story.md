## Story

**As Judith and her authorized assistant,**

**I want** premium operating intelligence that explains relationship health, pipeline movement, source progression and task follow-through from the same stored evidence,

**so that** we can choose the next useful action without trusting inflated conversion, decorative finance or disconnected dashboards.

**Traceability:** Story 3.3 event-backed funnel and CLI/UI parity; Story 3.9 responsive evidence correction; Story 3.23 canonical cadence/task direction; Story 3.24 actionable Pipeline; `docs/architecture/realtor-operating-system-incremental-architecture.md` dashboard evidence contract; authenticated Insights audit evidence captured on 2026-08-22.

**Dependencies:** Stories 3.0, 3.1, 3.2 and 3.3 are the workspace, task/activity, contact and analytics foundations. Story 3.23 remains the authority for canonical cadence materialization; this story may report only task evidence that is actually stored and must not claim Story 3.23's remaining work is complete.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Same-contact conversion never exceeds 100%.** Conversion uses distinct contact IDs, not transition-event counts. The denominator is the distinct contacts with an evidenced move out of `new` during the selected window; the numerator is the subset of those same contacts with a later evidenced move to `closed` inside that window. Each contact contributes at most once to each side, event order is enforced, the numerator cannot exceed the denominator, and the displayed percentage is therefore within 0–100%. A zero denominator produces `insufficient evidence`, not `0%`.
2. **30/90/365-day windows include a prior-period comparison.** Insights supports exactly 30, 90 and 365 days, defaults deterministically, and discloses current start/end, equal-length immediately preceding start/end and as-of time. Conversion, progression, transition and completed-task measures compare against the prior period when equivalent stored history exists. Current-snapshot measures are labelled as current and are not given fabricated historical deltas when no point-in-time source exists.
3. **Every headline and operational metric is actionable.** Contact totals, attention queues, portfolio segments, pipeline stages/aging, conversion cohorts, source progression and task rhythm provide a keyboard-operable drilldown to the exact authorized contributing records or to a clearly filtered operational surface. The drilldown preserves the selected period where relevant, states its filter/scope and does not lead to an unrelated generic page or a dead control.
4. **Portfolio health is evidence-backed and not a hidden score.** The view reports the current Hot, Warm and Nurture distribution plus actionable overdue follow-up, never-contacted and needs-qualification queues from canonical contact/cadence facts. Counts reconcile to the authorized active-contact scope; definitions and exclusions are visible; no opaque health score, predictive ranking or copied dashboard membership is introduced.
5. **Pipeline aging distinguishes current shape from historical evidence.** All seven canonical relationship stages show current distinct-contact count, evidenced-contact count and average days since the latest valid stage transition for contacts with history. Missing transition history renders aging unavailable rather than zero. Stage and aging drilldowns open the same Pipeline/contact evidence, and relationship pipeline state is never described as deal, contract, closing or financial authority.
6. **Source progression is useful but is not ROI.** For every stored lead source with evidence, the report shows distinct contacts, distinct contacts that reached `appointment-set`, `active`, `under-contract` or `closed`, and the resulting same-contact progression percentage within 0–100%. The definition and contributing records are available. The UI and CLI do not label this as revenue, profitability, return on investment or source ROI because spend and verified transaction/financial facts are unavailable.
7. **Task rhythm covers owner and assistant truthfully.** For the workspace and selected current/prior windows, the report shows open, overdue, due in the next seven days, completed, due-work completion and on-time completion from stored task timestamps/statuses. It breaks open, overdue and completed work down by active owner/assistant membership without rankings or unsupported productivity claims. Unassigned, inactive or unavailable membership attribution is disclosed as such rather than silently assigned or omitted; missing canonical cadence tasks remain `insufficient evidence`.
8. **Data coverage is explicit for every report.** UI and CLI expose schema/definition version, workspace/mode (`live-authenticated` or clearly labelled sample), as-of time, selected and prior bounds, loaded contact/event/task counts, each read cap, whether a cap was reached, active/archive inclusion rules and any omitted or insufficient source. A truncated source marks affected metrics incomplete and never presents them as full-workspace truth.
9. **Loading, empty, insufficient-evidence and error states are complete.** Initial and period-change loading identifies that authorized contacts, tasks and pipeline evidence are being read without exposing stale totals as current. Empty and partial data explain what is missing. A route/read failure states that no CRM data changed, preserves or can retry the selected period, offers a safe path back to work and does not silently substitute sample data for a failed authenticated read.
10. **The information design is accessible without relying on shape, color or motion.** Headings, regions, lists/tables, links and period navigation are semantic; charts have equivalent text values/definitions; focus order and visible focus are logical; all actions work by keyboard; status/error updates are announced appropriately; contrast and effective targets meet WCAG 2.1 AA expectations; native 200% zoom, reduced motion and screen-reader review preserve all data and drilldowns.
11. **The premium layout is responsive at 390, 768 and 1440 pixels.** At 390×844, 768×1024 and 1440×1000 the page has no document-level horizontal overflow, clipped labels/values, overlapping fixed shell/assistant elements or precision-only horizontal gestures. Dense lists and visualizations reflow or use an explicitly labelled contained scroller while the selected period, definitions, coverage and actions remain reachable.
12. **CLI and UI use one operating-intelligence contract.** `npm run crm -- insights --period <30|90|365>` (with the existing authenticated `--live` boundary) returns a deterministic, machine-readable versioned report containing the same current/prior bounds, cohort conversion, portfolio, pipeline aging, source progression, task rhythm, definitions and coverage used by `/insights`. Existing explicit `--from/--to` support may remain for diagnostics, but equivalent workspace, resolved window and fixed as-of input must produce matching aggregates. Invalid periods/dates fail with a non-zero safe error and no partial success envelope.
13. **Unavailable finance remains explicitly unavailable.** Volume, GCI, commission, expenses, forecast, profit/loss and source ROI are absent from operational totals and shown only as an evidence-protected unavailable state explaining that verified deal, transaction, commission and expense authorities do not yet exist. Missing finance is never rendered as `$0`, `0%`, estimated revenue or inferred from contact pipeline stages.
14. **Automated and runtime evidence covers the whole contract.** Unit tests cover distinct-contact cohort sequencing, duplicate/replayed/out-of-order events, 0%/100% bounds, insufficient evidence, prior periods, source progression, task timing/member attribution and truncation. Application/repository tests cover workspace isolation, active/archive rules, fixed-time determinism and sample/live parity. Mounted/browser tests cover drilldowns, period state, loading/error/empty/dense states, keyboard/screen-reader alternatives, reduced motion, 200% zoom and 390/768/1440 layouts.
15. **Quality gates pass before review or release.** `npm run lint`, `npm run typecheck`, `npm test` and `npm run build` pass; focused CLI parity smoke passes in deterministic sample mode and live-authenticated mode when configured; relevant database/RLS tests pass if repository/query contracts change; CodeRabbit reports no unresolved CRITICAL issue; independent QA records the responsive/accessibility/state matrix separately from deployment or production claims.

## Dev Notes

### Existing behavior and correction boundary

- The current domain report is `pipeline-analytics.v1`. It derives conversion numerator and denominator from transition-event counts, so repeated moves by one contact can make the numerator unrelated to the denominator. Story 3.25 changes the conversion unit to ordered distinct-contact cohorts while retaining stored activity events as evidence. [Source: `packages/crm/lib/domain/pipeline-analytics.ts`]
- The current application service defaults to 90 days, reads contacts plus at most 500 activity events, and does not load tasks or the equal-length prior period. Extend this application/repository seam rather than querying directly from the page. [Source: `packages/crm/lib/application/pipeline-analytics-service.ts`]
- `npm run crm -- insights` already exposes sample/live-authenticated JSON with `--from`/`--to`. Evolve that command and its versioned envelope; do not create a UI-only calculator or a second CLI. [Source: `packages/crm/scripts/manage-crm.ts`; `packages/crm/package.json`]
- The canonical task facts already include `dueAt`, status, assignee membership, `completedAt` and completing membership; task reads are bounded and workspace-scoped. Use those facts for task rhythm and do not infer completion or attribution from activity-card presentation. [Source: `packages/crm/lib/domain/activity.ts`; `packages/crm/lib/data/activity-repository.ts`; `packages/crm/lib/application/activity-commands.ts`]
- The repository context already exposes contact, activity, workspace and live/sample scope seams. Live authenticated read failure must fail closed; the current signed-out transitional sample path is not authorization to replace failed live Insights data silently. [Source: `packages/crm/lib/data/index.ts`]
- `buildWorkspaceSnapshot` provides deterministic current contact, pipeline, source and readiness facts but does not provide historical snapshots. Reuse its canonical definitions where appropriate and label snapshot metrics honestly. [Source: `packages/crm/lib/domain/workspace-intelligence.ts`]
