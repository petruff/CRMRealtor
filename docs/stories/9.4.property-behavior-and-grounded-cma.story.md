## Story

As a Florida realtor, I need authorized property behavior and CMA work to remain cited and governed, so that Omnix can propose timely follow-up and organize comparable research without inventing market value or silently changing a relationship.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Inquiry, view, saved, favorite, showing-request, and seller-intake events retain contact/property/source/time, permission, idempotency, and append-only evidence.
2. Repeat-view summaries derive only from exact events and remain proposals; behavior never directly changes lead type, pipeline, transaction, task, or property facts.
3. A CMA request records subject property, seller/contact, purpose, exact selection criteria, requester, as-of time, provider authority, state, and explicit unknowns.
4. CMA remains blocked when licensed comparables are unavailable, expired, revoked, or outside permitted use.
5. Comparable candidates retain source, provider record, distance/date/type/size criteria, included/excluded decision, reason, and citation.
6. No model or calculation may invent valuation, range, sale, listing, tax, insurance, or comparable facts.
7. Owner/assistant authority, workspace isolation, optimistic state, replay, recovery, and append-only evidence fail closed.
8. Empty, blocked, pending, ready, completed, expired, and revoked states are human-readable on desktop/mobile.
9. Migration, rollback/forward repair, RLS/RPC, domain/repository/UI, accessibility, and recovery tests pass locally.
10. Live comparable retrieval remains Conditional on Story 9.3 provider authority and real licensed UAT.
