## Story

**As a** realtor or authorized assistant,  
**I want** contact-linked Gmail operations and Omnix follow-up tasks reconciled to a dedicated Google Calendar,  
**so that** communication and due work stay visible without password capture, overbroad access or duplicate sends/events.

**Traceability:** RQ-08, RQ-09 and RQ-21; D-11, L-15, L-28, L-29, B-08 and B-14 in the parity roadmap/matrix.

**Dependencies:** Story 3.5 owns connector OAuth, encrypted tokens, durable jobs, approvals, cursors, receipts and disconnect. Story 3.1 owns Omnix tasks/activities and remains the calendar-side authority. Story 3.2 owns canonical email contact points used for conservative Gmail matching. Supabase Google sign-in remains identity-only and is not connector authorization.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **CLI-first Google connection with guided owner consent.** Documented CLI commands initiate/complete Google connector authorization, request each feature bundle incrementally, inspect exact granted/required scopes and remote account, run safe probes/sync/reconcile and disconnect. The owner UI may aggregate the three already reviewed operational scopes into one explicit `workspace-core` consent screen; persisted runtime authority remains the three independent capability rows and every action still fails closed on its exact scope. Existing sign-in continues to request only `openid email profile`; Gmail and Calendar authorization is a separate, owner-initiated Story 3.5 OAuth transaction. Omnix never captures a Google password or app password.
2. **Least-privilege bundles and truthful capability.** Initial implementation requests only `gmail.send` for approved email send, `gmail.metadata` for the approved header/label activity JTBD, and `calendar.app.created` for an Omnix-created secondary calendar. Each bundle is independently consented, persisted and displayed. Missing/denied/revoked scope disables only dependent actions and returns reauthorization guidance. No `gmail.readonly`, `gmail.modify`, broad `calendar.events`, body access or primary-calendar write is added without a separately approved story and current official-scope review.
3. **Workspace/account and assistant authority.** The owner connects, expands scopes, chooses/replaces the Google account and disconnects. The remote account identity is bound to the workspace/connection and checked during callback/reconnect. Assistants never read tokens or broaden scopes; they may create/edit an Omnix email draft or task under workspace policy, but external email/calendar writes require the Story 3.5 owner approval unless a later approved policy explicitly changes that boundary.
4. **Conservative Gmail activity matching.** Gmail metadata synchronization stores only the approved minimized activity projection: provider message/thread IDs, direction, participant addresses needed for matching, timestamp, labels and a provider link; bodies/attachments are not requested. Subject persistence is off unless the approved privacy/retention decision explicitly allows encrypted CRM storage. Exact normalized counterpart email must resolve to exactly one active Story 3.2 contact point. Shared, multiple, archived, group-address, self-only or otherwise ambiguous messages enter an unresolved review state and never auto-link.
5. **Gmail cursor and history recovery.** The connection persists private history/cursor/watch state. Push/watch callbacks are verified wake-ups only; bounded `history.list` reconciliation is authoritative. Initial sync is range/bound limited; incremental results are idempotent. Expired history state triggers an explicit bounded resync, not silent data loss. Duplicate/out-of-order callbacks, worker restarts and replay cannot duplicate contact activities. Sync state, lag, last success and redacted failure are visible.
6. **Compose/send inside Omnix with real evidence.** A user creates an Omnix-local draft against an exact contact/email point, previews recipients/content and creates a versioned Story 3.5 action intent. The owner approves the exact current payload before `gmail.send`. Provider acceptance records the Gmail message/thread identity and redacted receipt, then contact activity. Timeout/ambiguous acceptance enters reconciliation and is never blindly resent. Edit, recipient drift, scope loss, stale approval or archived contact returns to pending/refused. A local draft/status is never called sent.
7. **Omnix task authority and dedicated calendar.** On owner approval, Omnix creates one named secondary calendar through `calendar.app.created`; its ID is workspace/connection-bound. Story 3.1 task ID/version/due time is canonical. Eligible create/update/complete/cancel operations use a stable provider resource key and reconcile to one Omnix-owned event. Remote edits/deletions never silently overwrite the task; they create a conflict/reconciliation outcome according to an owner-visible policy. No unrelated or primary-calendar event is read or changed.
8. **Calendar synchronization and recovery.** Initial plus incremental event synchronization uses a private `syncToken`, bounded pages and documented full-resync handling when the token expires. Duplicate task events, overlapping workers, 429/5xx, revoked scope, deleted calendar/event and timezone/DST changes are reconciled idempotently with receipts. Event time uses the workspace/task timezone contract; unknown/invalid timezone blocks write rather than guessing. Completion/cancellation behavior is versioned and tested.
9. **Privacy, receipts and disconnect.** Provider tokens, OAuth codes/state, message bodies, subjects (unless separately approved), recipient addresses and event description content never enter logs or Story 3.5 receipts. Content storage/retention/deletion and assistant visibility require the approved policy before live sync. Disconnect blocks new operations, revokes the Google grant where supported, cryptoshreds local token material, stops watches, preserves CRM activity/task history and reports confirmed versus unconfirmed revocation honestly.
10. **Truthful UX, tests and real-account UAT.** `/connections`, contact timeline/compose and task/calendar surfaces separately show identity sign-in, Gmail send, Gmail metadata and Calendar capability, exact account/scopes, sync health and blocked/degraded states. Tests cover two workspaces/roles, OAuth state/PKCE/account swap, incremental scopes, matching ambiguity, cursor expiry, duplicate callbacks, send ambiguity/no-resend, stable calendar resources, task/remote conflict, timezone, redaction and disconnect. CLI smoke, database tests, desktop/390px accessibility, lint, typecheck, full tests and build pass. Real UAT proves incremental consent, one send, one matched received/sent metadata activity, one task create/update/delete reconciliation and revoke/failure before a live claim.

## Out of Scope

- Primary-calendar or unrelated-event management, arbitrary user-selected calendar scopes or status-only send/event success.
- Reusing Supabase identity tokens, browser refresh tokens, service-account domain-wide delegation or capturing Google credentials.

### Provider contract and configuration

- Deployment config keeps Google disabled and defines `GOOGLE_CONNECTOR_CLIENT_ID`, `GOOGLE_CONNECTOR_CLIENT_SECRET`, the three initial bundles/actions and their idempotency modes. Values remain server secrets. [Source: `docs/architecture/config/connector-platform.example.yaml#providers.google`]
- Google classifies `gmail.send` as sensitive and `gmail.metadata` as restricted; `gmail.metadata` permits headers/labels but not bodies. `calendar.app.created` permits a secondary calendar and its events. Scope/verification status must be rechecked at T0. [Official Gmail scopes](https://developers.google.com/workspace/gmail/api/auth/scopes) · [Official Calendar scopes](https://developers.google.com/workspace/calendar/api/auth)]
- Gmail history and Calendar sync tokens require initial plus incremental synchronization and explicit expired-token recovery. [Official Gmail sync](https://developers.google.com/workspace/gmail/api/guides/sync) · [Official Calendar sync](https://developers.google.com/workspace/calendar/api/guides/sync)
- `docs/accumulated-context.md` is absent; use the roadmap's documented fallback and the named Done/Ready stories.
