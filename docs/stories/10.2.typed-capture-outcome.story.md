## Story

As a realtor who has finished a call, meeting, showing, or appointment, I need to enter one short recap and receive a reviewable set of CRM updates and follow-up actions so that I can preserve context and complete administration without duplicate entry.

## User-visible outcome

The user selects **Capture outcome** from Today, Contact 360, a transaction, or a meeting brief. The first release accepts typed or pasted text. Audio and direct call recording remain separate capability gates.

Omnix returns one consolidated review containing:

- Source recap.
- Concise summary.
- Confirmed and uncertain buyer/seller intent.
- Preferences, exclusions, timeline, geography, budget, financing state, and constraints.
- Questions and objections.
- Commitments owned by the realtor and by the client.
- Proposed contact-field changes.
- Proposed note.
- Proposed tasks and due dates.
- Proposed appointment.
- Proposed pipeline or nurture transition.
- Proposed follow-up email or text draft.
- Compliance, contradiction, confidence, and missing-information flags.

The user can accept all safe items, select individual items, edit, reject, or defer. Nothing changes until the current exact proposal version is confirmed.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Authorized entry points.** Today, Contact 360, Transaction, and Meeting Brief use the same server-owned Capture Outcome command. Workspace and actor authority come from authenticated membership, never caller-supplied owner or workspace fields.
2. **Typed-first boundary.** Version 1 accepts typed or pasted recap text with explicit maximum size. Audio upload, recording, transcription, speaker separation, and recording consent are out of scope until Story 10.3.
3. **Untrusted input.** The recap is untrusted data. Existing Unicode, instruction-override, code, path, and injection guards run before model interpretation. A rejected recap is preserved only according to the approved retention policy and cannot invoke tools.
4. **Structured extraction.** The model returns a versioned schema. Invalid, extra, unsupported, or oversized fields fail validation and cannot enter the proposal system.
5. **Evidence spans.** Every extracted fact or action includes the exact source span or an explicit "not directly stated" state. Suggested follow-up language may be inferred but cannot be presented as a factual extraction.
6. **Confidence and contradiction.** Each extracted field exposes confidence and contradiction state. Dates, money, addresses, names, consent, commitments, and lifecycle transitions receive stricter confirmation treatment than descriptive notes.
7. **Negation and ownership.** Tests prove that "not ready," "do not text," "client will call," "I will send," "may list next year," and similar negation, uncertainty, subject, and timing differences are not collapsed.
8. **Canonical truth protection.** Extraction creates a proposal; it never directly changes contact, property, transaction, task, appointment, pipeline, nurture, consent, or provider state.
9. **Consolidated proposal.** One parent CaptureOutcomeProposal contains typed child operations with stable order, exact targets, before/after preview, evidence, rationale, confidence, preconditions, and item-level selection.
10. **Edit and versioning.** Editing any item creates a new immutable proposal version and content hash. Confirmation applies only to the exact current version and selected items.
11. **Partial approval.** The user can approve safe items individually. A failure in one selected child operation does not falsely mark another as complete; the parent shows per-item receipt and recovery state.
12. **Operation allowlist.** Initial typed operations are limited to append note, propose contact preference update, create task, create appointment intent, move relationship pipeline, transition nurture, prepare Gmail draft, and prepare SMS draft where the channel is eligible.
13. **Consent authority.** Extracted consent or opt-out language is always surfaced for review with source span. Existing authoritative suppression rules run before a communication draft can be approved or executed. The model cannot grant channel eligibility.
14. **Dates and money.** The model may extract exact stated values. Deterministic validators handle calendar validity, timezone, amount format, and domain rules. Ambiguous dates require clarification and never default silently.
15. **Lifecycle transitions.** Pipeline and nurture changes show the current state, proposed state, source phrase, reason, and consequences. Transaction status or deadlines are not changed by this story.
16. **Draft quality.** A proposed follow-up uses authorized relationship facts and the approved voice profile, acknowledges the actual conversation, includes no invented promises, and passes Fair Housing and channel policy lint.
17. **Fallback.** When the model is unconfigured, over budget, timed out, or failed, the user can still save the recap as a note and manually add tasks from the same workflow.
18. **Idempotency.** Re-submitting the same source and idempotency key returns the same proposal. Re-confirming an executed child returns the original receipt and performs no duplicate mutation or provider handoff.
19. **Concurrency.** Source or target changes after proposal creation mark affected items stale. Unaffected items may remain reviewable if the architecture proves independent preconditions.
20. **Recovery.** Pending, selected, approved, executing, completed, partially completed, failed, stale, expired, rejected, and deferred states are explicit and recoverable.
21. **Retention.** Raw recap retention, redaction, export, deletion, and audit behavior follow the approved data policy. Logs never contain full recap text, hidden prompts, secrets, or unrestricted provider content.
22. **Responsive review.** Mobile presents grouped proposal cards with visible before/after changes and sticky but unobscuring review controls. Desktop may use a split evidence/diff view. Neither requires horizontal scrolling.
23. **Accessibility.** Item selection, edit, expand, confirm, error, and success states are keyboard and screen-reader operable, visibly focused, announced, and usable at 200-percent zoom with reduced motion.
24. **Evaluation.** The release corpus covers names, phone/email, budget, financing, address, timezones, relative and ambiguous dates, commitments, negation, opt-out, protected-class content, steering requests, objections, profanity, mixed language, prompt injection, unsupported operations, cross-workspace IDs, timeout, invalid JSON, and model refusal.
25. **Release evidence.** Lint, TypeScript, focused tests, full tests, build, migration/RLS if added, authenticated owner/assistant UAT, and 390/768/1440 browser evidence pass on the exact candidate.

## Proposed interaction

### Step 1 — Capture

- Context header: contact, meeting, time, and optional related transaction.
- Input modes: Type or paste; audio appears only when Story 10.3 is enabled.
- Clear privacy and source label.
- Primary action: Review outcome.
- Secondary action: Save as note without AI.

### Step 2 — Process

Show real states only:

- Checking input.
- Extracting.
- Validating.
- Comparing with CRM.
- Preparing review.

A user may leave and return. The run is persisted and discoverable.

### Step 3 — Review

Group items:

1. Summary and note.
2. Facts and preferences.
3. Commitments and tasks.
4. Appointment.
5. Relationship workflow.
6. Follow-up draft.
7. Risks and questions.

Each item shows:

- Proposed change.
- Exact source phrase.
- Confidence or ambiguity.
- Existing value when applicable.
- Edit control.
- Include/exclude control.
- Consequence and required authority.

### Step 4 — Confirm

The confirmation screen lists only selected items, current version, communication recipient/channel, and any required owner-only authority.

### Step 5 — Result

Show per-item results:

- Completed with receipt.
- Draft prepared.
- Awaiting separate provider confirmation.
- Failed with recovery.
- Skipped.
- Stale and requiring refresh.

Finish with the resulting Next Best Action.

## Success measures

Measure baseline before targets:

- Median time from conversation end to saved CRM outcome.
- Proposal completion rate.
- Child-operation acceptance, edit, rejection, defer, stale, and failure rates.
- Correction rate by extracted field category.
- Percentage of accepted outcomes creating a valid next action.
- Follow-up draft approval and provider completion rate.
- Duplicate task or communication rate.
- User-reported incorrect extraction.
- Seven-day repeat use.
- Model and provider cost per completed outcome.

## Non-goals

- Direct call recording, transcription, speaker separation, or audio storage.
- Silent contact, pipeline, nurture, transaction, task, appointment, or provider mutation.
- Inferring consent from relationship or channel history.
- Automatically changing transaction terms or legal deadlines.
- Protected-class profiling, steering, or targeting.
- Legal, lending, appraisal, insurance, tax, school-quality, safety, or investment advice.
- Treating a draft or action intent as sent or completed.
- Free-form model access to tools, SQL, providers, secrets, or unrestricted CRM data.

## Definition of done

The story is done only when an authorized owner and assistant can enter the same representative recap on desktop and mobile; receive a source-linked, confidence-aware, editable proposal; approve selected items without duplicate or cross-workspace effects; preserve a usable manual fallback; recover from stale or partial failure; and verify every completed operation through canonical or provider receipts on the exact release candidate.
