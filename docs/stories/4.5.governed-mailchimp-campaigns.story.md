## Story

**As a** realtor workspace owner,
**I want** to prepare and approve a Mailchimp campaign for all eligible subscribed contacts or a selected CRM segment,
**so that** I can communicate at scale without manually copying contacts or sending marketing email to opted-out recipients.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Gmail remains a one-to-one communication channel. Bulk marketing email uses the workspace's selected Mailchimp audience and never fans out through Gmail.
2. A CLI-first campaign preview returns a versioned, machine-readable envelope with audience, segment, eligible recipient count, exclusion counts by reason, subject, preview text, content hash, actor, workspace and as-of time. It contains no raw recipient list by default.
3. Eligible recipients are limited to unique Mailchimp audience members whose current provider status is `subscribed`. `unsubscribed`, `non-subscribed`, `cleaned`, `pending`, missing email, duplicates and unresolved sync records are excluded and visibly counted. CRM facts cannot override an unsubscribe.
4. The owner or assistant may prepare and edit a campaign draft. Only the workspace owner may approve the exact current version for provider execution. Content, audience, segment, recipient snapshot or connection drift invalidates approval and requires a new preview.
5. Campaign creation and sending are separate governed actions. Approval of a draft does not imply send approval. No operation is reported as sent without a provider receipt and reconciliation.
6. The Mailchimp adapter supports bounded campaign draft creation, content update, preflight/readiness inspection and send for an exact campaign ID. Provider tokens remain server-only; provider errors are categorized without exposing response bodies or contact data.
7. The campaign editor supports all eligible subscribers plus Hot, Warm and Nurture segments. It shows included/excluded counts before approval, provides subject, preview text, from/reply-to identity and accessible HTML/plain-text content, and remains usable at 390px.
8. Omnix may propose subject lines, content and a target segment from cited CRM facts, but it cannot add recipients, approve or send. Model output is untrusted draft content and passes injection/content limits before persistence.
9. Every preview, revision, approval, provider call, receipt, reconciliation result and failure is workspace-scoped, idempotent and auditable. Assistant and owner permissions, revoked membership and two-workspace isolation are tested.
10. Existing individual Gmail drafts, Mailchimp audience synchronization, unsubscribe webhook handling and connector recovery remain backward compatible. Lint, typecheck, full tests and build pass before release.

## Out of Scope

- Sending marketing email to every CRM contact regardless of subscription status.
- Bulk Gmail fan-out, BCC blasts, purchased lists or guessed consent.
- Autonomous AI approval or send.
- Claiming delivery, opens, clicks or conversion without provider evidence.
