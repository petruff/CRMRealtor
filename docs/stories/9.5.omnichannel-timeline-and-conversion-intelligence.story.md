# Story 9.5 — Omnichannel Timeline and Conversion Intelligence

**Epic:** 9 — Property Intelligence and Lead Conversion
**Status:** Locally Complete — Provider UAT Conditional
**Owner:** @architect + @dev + @data-engineer
**Quality Gate:** @qa + @ux-design-expert

## Story

As a realtor, I need one human-readable relationship timeline with source, consent, response, and conversion evidence, so that I can understand what happened and what is late without reading provider receipts or internal event names.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. CRM, email, text, website, property behavior, attribution, consent, task, and response-SLA evidence project into one ordered, bounded timeline without copying restricted content.
2. Each entry identifies channel, direction/state, human label, safe detail, source time, and evidence authority.
3. Consent remains channel-specific; one channel never grants another channel permission.
4. Response timers derive from canonical website SLA/task evidence and label due, overdue, responded, cancelled, or unavailable without invented deadlines.
5. Stage aging derives from the latest canonical pipeline transition or explicitly labeled record fallback.
6. Source conversion resolves to exact attribution evidence and never credits an unknown source.
7. Disabled/unavailable provider channels degrade to absent or explicit unavailable state without breaking CRM history.
8. Internal IDs, hashes, raw payloads, OAuth scopes, connector event keys, and deterministic grammar are not displayed to Judith.
9. Projection, repository queries, responsive component, accessibility, and negative-path tests pass.
10. Provider-backed completeness remains Conditional on each enabled connector's real authorization and reconciliation evidence.
