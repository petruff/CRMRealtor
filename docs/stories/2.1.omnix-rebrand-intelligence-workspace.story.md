# Story 2.1 — Omnix Rebrand & Intelligence Workspace

**Epic:** 2 — Omnix Product Expansion  
**Status:** Done  
**Owner:** @dev (Vulcan)  
**Created:** 2026-08-11 by @sm (Chronos)

## Story

**As a** realtor operating with occasional assistant support,  
**I want** one clearly branded workspace that shows my pipeline, business health and explainable priorities,  
**so that** I stay organized without adopting another cluttered dashboard or trusting unsupported AI claims.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Every shipped product surface, metadata value and package identity uses **Omnix** and visibly attributes **Powered by Cyryx Labs**; generic references to the realtor profession may remain.
2. Existing Today, Contacts, Mailers and Connections routes and capabilities remain available. The Apple-inspired tokens, typography, focus, reduced motion and light/dark behavior are preserved.
3. Brand metadata is centralized. The shell uses the existing app icon as a real asset rather than a letter placeholder; no unapproved palette, logo or marketing claim is invented.
4. A pure, bounded workspace-intelligence module computes pipeline, source, data-readiness and explainable recommendation summaries from contacts. A JSON CLI exposes the same summary without React/browser imports and has deterministic tests.
5. `/pipeline` presents every contact exactly once by stage, preserves a stable real-estate stage order, links to the source record and remains usable without drag-and-drop.
6. `/insights` reports only evidenced contact/pipeline/source/readiness metrics. It must not fabricate revenue, conversion, commissions or market data.
7. `/omnix` presents a read-only daily intelligence brief with evidence and direct safe navigation. It explicitly states that no generative model or autonomous external action is connected.
8. New workspaces are discoverable on desktop and through an additive mobile workspace hub while the existing four mobile destinations remain intact.
9. Full lint, typecheck, tests, optimized build, CLI smoke, desktop and 390px runtime, both themes, semantic navigation and zero console errors pass.

## Out of Scope

- Copying or modifying proprietary RealtorsPal/Realdash implementation or assets.
- Live Gmail, Calendar, Mailchimp, Twilio, Meta or MLS authorization.
- Generative AI inference, autonomous messaging or provider credentials.
- Listings, transactions, commissions, P&L, custom fields or database migrations.
- Publishing, deployment, production account creation or branch changes.
