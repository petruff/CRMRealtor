## Story

As a Florida realtor, I need every verified transaction deadline to retain its source, responsibility, history, and risk state, so Omnix can warn me early without inventing legal obligations.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Milestone catalog supports inspection, financing, appraisal, title, contingency, association, flood, closing, and custom kinds without presenting templates as legal advice.
2. Every milestone retains source type, source reference, source date, due time and timezone, responsible membership, current state, version, confidence/verification state, and append-only transitions.
3. Approaching, overdue, contradictory, missing-responsibility, and unverified dates materialize explainable attention records exactly once.
4. Today, Alerts, Approvals, contact, and transaction surfaces resolve to the same canonical milestone and attention occurrence.
5. Completing, waiving, cancelling, correcting, or reassigning a milestone requires current version and records actor, time, reason, and prior value.
6. A workflow suggestion may propose a milestone but cannot create a verified deadline without an authorized person confirming the exact date and source.
7. Calendar preparation remains governed and idempotent; provider uncertainty cannot mark a milestone synchronized without a receipt.
8. Unknown dates remain unknown and do not produce fabricated countdowns, business risk, or legal recommendations.
9. Desktop/mobile/PWA views keep urgent dates legible, filterable, keyboard accessible, and free from date-control overflow.
10. RLS, replay, concurrency, timezone, DST, contradiction, accessibility, and recovery suites pass with mandatory quality gates.
