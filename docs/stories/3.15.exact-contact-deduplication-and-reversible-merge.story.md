# Story 3.15 — Exact Contact Deduplication & Reversible Merge

**Epic:** 3 — CRM operating foundation
**Status:** InProgress
**Owner:** @data-engineer (Ceres) with @dev (Vulcan)
**Quality Gate:** @qa (Argus)
**Created:** 2026-08-14 by @sm (Chronos)

## Story

**As an Omnix workspace owner,**
**I want** to audit and safely consolidate contacts proven to be duplicates,
**so that** Judith has one complete client record without losing notes, activity, consent, assignments, provider links or provenance.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. A CLI-first read-only audit reports active/archived counts and duplicate candidate groups by exact normalized external provider ID, canonical email or canonical E.164 phone, with no raw PII in console, logs or receipts.
2. Names, cities, fuzzy similarity, household membership and lead type never establish duplicate identity. Conflicting exact identifiers produce review-only candidates and zero mutation.
3. Automatic consolidation is limited to a zero-conflict group with one deterministic survivor selected by: externally linked record first, then oldest created record, then stable UUID order. A dry-run plan precedes every mutation.
4. The merge transaction revalidates the snapshot, workspace, actor role and exact identity evidence; stale/divergent plans fail closed. It never accepts browser-supplied owner/workspace authority.
5. Consolidation preserves or relinks all compatible contact points, external links, notes, activity events, tasks, households, relationships, assignments, custom values, incomplete-record links and provider records. Conflicting single-valued facts are retained on the survivor or reported for review; none are silently overwritten.
6. Duplicate records are archived with merge reason, survivor reference, actor/time and immutable redacted receipt. They are not hard-deleted. The operation is idempotent and an exact replay is a no-op.
7. A rollback/recovery contract is documented and tested. If full automatic reversal cannot be guaranteed for a relationship, the merge remains review-only rather than partially applying.
8. Production cleanup executes only after migration/TAP, repository tests, a fresh read-only production audit and a backup/export receipt. The final report exposes aggregate counts, merged groups, review-only groups and unchanged contacts without PII.
9. Existing import identity resolution by external ID, canonical email and canonical phone remains unchanged and prevents new duplicates; regression covers shared identifiers, archived matches and exact replay.
10. Supabase reset/migration, focused pgTAP, RLS/service-role checks, CLI dry-run, lint, typecheck, full tests and build pass before production mutation.

## Dev Notes

- Canonical identity sources are `contact_external_links` and active/archived `contact_points`; reuse the normalization and outcomes of `resolve_contact_import_identity(...)` from migration 0007.
- `contacts.email`, `phone` and `secondary_phone` are legacy projections, not independent merge authority.
- Existing `archive_contact(...)` is insufficient by itself because many tables use restrictive foreign keys. Inventory and transaction proof are required before mutation.
- No PII may be emitted by the CLI report. Candidate references use group hash plus contact UUIDs only.
- The approved merge shape is a group-scoped logical alias star: one database-selected survivor and one or more donor members. Physical contact/dependent rows are never reparented; alias chains, partial reversal and already-aliased candidate groups are review-only in v1.
- T3 is split into an additive 0027 foundation and a forward-only activation migration. The foundation may ship plan/read/guard contracts, but owner apply remains activation-gated until every contact-scoped repository and outbound dispatch path is alias-aware.
- Contact reads aggregate `list_contact_alias_group_ids(...)`; writes resolve to the survivor. The original physical `contact_id` remains on notes, activity, source facts and provider evidence so provenance and whole-plan reversal do not require reconstruction.
- A stale plan is detected from a canonical digest of member IDs, identity/archive state, dependency fingerprints, alias epoch and in-flight outbound authority—not dependency counts alone. Apply locks the plan and member contacts in UUID order, recomputes the digest and fails closed on any difference.
- Outbound review and worker dispatch both call `assert_contact_outbound_target(...)`. A donor, donor-owned point or changed alias epoch becomes manual review; the system never silently substitutes a recipient after approval.
- Import identity resolution maps raw exact matches through active aliases before ambiguity evaluation. One canonical result targets the active survivor and records only a hash of the original physical match set; reversal restores the existing archived-match behavior.
- Architecture readiness: GO for T3 foundation behind the activation gate and T4 dry-run; NO-GO for production apply/CLI `--apply` until repository aggregation, import compatibility, connector dispatch guards, pgTAP and whole-plan reverse tests are green.
