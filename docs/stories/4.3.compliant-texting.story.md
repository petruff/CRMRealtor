## Story

**As a** realtor or authorized assistant,  
**I want** consent-aware provider-backed texting recorded with each contact,  
**so that** I can communicate from Omnix while opt-outs, quiet hours, delivery evidence and carrier approval are enforced.

**Traceability:** RQ-20; D-09, D-10, S-07 and B-07 in the parity roadmap/matrix; texting target accepted by the Product Owner. Voice is not evidenced as a current client requirement and is excluded.

**Dependencies:** Story 3.5 supplies restricted server credentials, durable jobs, owner approvals, signed webhook ingress, receipts and reconciliation. Story 3.2 supplies canonical phone contact points and consent facts. Story 3.0 supplies owner/assistant authority.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **CLI-first service and readiness.** CLI commands inspect the configured Twilio account/Messaging Service/sender in redacted form, registration and compliance gates, signed-callback health, consent status, eligible conversations/jobs/receipts, bounded reconciliation and disconnect/disable. The Twilio service uses Omnix-owned server credentials; there is no consumer-login or password capture. UI follows the same application services after CLI proof.
2. **Fail-closed activation.** Production `message.send` is unavailable unless deployment configuration, restricted API credential, owned sender/Messaging Service, callback URL/signature validation, applicable carrier registration, approved use case/message flow, consent/STOP/HELP/quiet-hours policy and real-number test evidence are all present. Sandbox/deterministic adapters are labelled non-live. Pending/rejected/expired registration, trial restrictions or missing evidence produce an honest blocked state and preserve the current device `sms:` fallback as a separate non-provider action.
3. **Workspace/sender/account authority.** The service connection and sender/Messaging Service bind to exactly one workspace; owner alone configures/disables it or changes policy. Assistants never read credentials, alter sender/registration/compliance policy or self-approve an external send. Worker scope comes from the claimed job, not browser-provided workspace/account/sender values. Cross-workspace phone, conversation, job, callback and receipt access fails.
4. **Consent evidence and contact identity.** Every outbound recipient is an exact active canonical Story 3.2 phone point. Consent is never inferred from a phone number, prior contact, imported tag or inbound message. The channel/use-case-specific record includes status (`unknown | opted_in | opted_out`), collection source/method, disclosure/policy version, actor/evidence reference and timestamps. Missing/invalid/expired consent or an archived/ambiguous/shared phone blocks provider send. Fresh re-opt-in is a new evidence event; old opt-out history remains append-only.
5. **Compose, approval and quiet-hours decision.** A user creates an Omnix-local message draft, selects the exact contact/phone and approved use case, previews content/cost disclosure and creates a versioned Story 3.5 action intent. Owner approval binds recipient, sender, body hash, consent snapshot, policy version, timezone and scheduled decision. The application applies the approved federal/state/provider quiet-hours policy using the best verified recipient timezone; an unknown timezone blocks or defers according to the approved fail-closed policy, never guesses. Any edit or drift requires reapproval.
6. **Durable send and no duplicate side effect.** Approved work queues exactly once and calls the configured provider through Story 3.5. A returned Message SID/provider acceptance persists a redacted receipt and conversation activity. A timeout after possible acceptance enters provider lookup/reconciliation, not blind retry. Duplicate drains/restarts and stale workers cannot create a second message. Validation, consent, opt-out, registration, policy and permission failures are terminal until the underlying evidence changes.
7. **Signed inbound messages and STOP/HELP/START.** Inbound/status callbacks are validated with the official Twilio SDK against exact raw parameters and externally visible URL before mutation, bound to the configured account/sender and replay-deduplicated. STOP/opt-out is durably applied before any other message processing and blocks queued/not-started sends; HELP and START/re-opt-in follow the approved Messaging Service configuration and record evidence. Unsupported or ambiguous keywords go to human review. Duplicate/out-of-order callbacks cannot reverse opt-out or duplicate conversation events.
8. **Conversation content, delivery state and receipts.** Authorized contact history shows inbound/outbound content and state only under an approved encrypted storage/retention/deletion policy; message bodies never enter operational logs or Story 3.5 receipts. Receipts store SID, direction/status, timestamps, hashes and redacted error category. Delivery status transitions are monotonic; out-of-order callbacks cannot regress final state. Missing terminal callbacks are reconciled against the provider on a bounded schedule.
9. **Disable, retention and honest recovery.** Disabling the service stops new sends, cancels only pre-side-effect jobs, retains consent/opt-out/conversation/activity/receipt history under policy and removes/rotates local credentials as directed. Ambiguous sends remain visible until reconciled. The UI distinguishes `registration pending | approval blocked | configured disabled | active | degraded | reauthorization/credential required | disconnected` and never calls a queued/local message delivered.
10. **Security, compliance and real-number UAT.** Tests cover two workspaces/roles, service-key isolation, exact phone/consent matching, opt-out race/no-call, HELP/START, quiet hours/DST/unknown timezone, approval drift, duplicate jobs/webhooks, callback signature/external URL, out-of-order delivery, ambiguous send reconciliation, redaction, trial/registration blocked states and disable. CLI smoke, database tests, desktop/390px accessibility, lint, typecheck, full tests and build pass. Controlled real-number UAT proves opt-in → owner-approved send → delivery callback → reply → STOP blocks future sends → reconcile/disable before production activation.

## Out of Scope

- Voice/calling, emergency messaging, cold/unsolicited bulk messaging, generic marketing automation or evasion of carrier/provider controls.
- Legal certification: Omnix enforces the approved policy and evidence; counsel/business owners retain compliance responsibility.
- Treating the device `sms:` link, Twilio trial, deterministic fixture or local status as provider-backed delivery.
