# Story 6.9: Canonical owner and bounded support authority

## Status

Ready for Review

## Story

As Judith, I need to remain the only canonical workspace owner while the developer can provide audited support without assuming my provider identity, consent, or secrets.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Workspace scope preserves the authenticated membership role and never rewrites an assistant to `owner`.
2. A separate, explicit support-grant capability permits only named administrative support operations; all unclassified operations fail closed.
3. Google and Mailchimp connect, callback, reconnect, disconnect, account/audience selection, revocation, and provider-bound approval require the authenticated canonical owner.
4. Workspace AI credentials, provider secrets, OAuth transactions, scopes, and revocation state require the authenticated canonical owner.
5. Database predicates and security-definer functions distinguish canonical ownership from support grants and verify the actor, membership, workspace, and owner identity transactionally.
6. Judith remains `owner / active`; the developer remains `assistant / active` with a separately auditable support grant.
