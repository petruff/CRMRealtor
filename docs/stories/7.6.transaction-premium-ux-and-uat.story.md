# Story 7.6 — Transaction Premium UX and UAT

**Epic:** 7 — Florida Realtor Operating System
**Status:** In Progress
**Owner:** @qa (Argus) with @ux-design-expert (Iris)
**Release Owner:** @devops (Polaris)

## Story

As Judith, I need the complete Florida transaction experience to be coherent, premium, accessible, and recoverable across desktop, iPhone Safari, and the installed PWA before it is released.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Representative buyer, seller, listing, lease/referral, condo/association, flood, closing, financial, affordability, and missing-data journeys are scripted with expected facts and outcomes.
2. Owner and clean-assistant journeys prove the same allow/deny matrix at UI, action, repository, RPC, and RLS boundaries.
3. Critical routes pass 390, 414, 768, and 1440 widths; iPhone 11 Safari and installed PWA cover create, edit, dates, approvals, recovery, offline/reconnect/update, theme, and logout.
4. Keyboard, focus, screen-reader semantics, 200% zoom, text spacing, contrast, touch targets, reduced motion, live regions, and error recovery pass.
5. No unbounded list, raw infrastructure record, clipped date/currency control, fixed-overlay collision, or hidden primary action remains.
6. Counts, deadlines, financial metrics, assumptions, and contributor drill-through reconcile to canonical records and as-of periods.
7. Console/network errors, provider degradation, stale versions, duplicate submissions, offline interruption, and uncertain outcomes produce safe recoverable states.
8. Migration replay, rollback/forward repair, backup/read-back, exact-SHA manifest, dependency/secret checks, lint, typecheck, full tests, and build pass on one candidate.
9. Any P0/P1 transaction, accessibility, security, data-integrity, recovery, or critical-journey defect blocks release.
10. QA and DevOps record separate implementation, automated verification, authenticated UAT, deployment, and production-smoke states.
