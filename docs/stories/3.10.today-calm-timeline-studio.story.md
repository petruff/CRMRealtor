# Story 3.10 — Today Calm Timeline Studio

**Epic:** 3 — Operational CRM Parity  
**Status:** InReview  
**Owner:** @dev

## Story

**As a** realtor or authorized assistant,  
**I want** Today to present the workday as one visual, calm and actionable timeline,  
**so that** I can understand the next priority within five seconds without scanning repetitive panels.

**Traceability:** User-selected Product Design option 3, “Calm Timeline Studio”; additive refinement of Done Story 3.8.  
**Visual target:** `C:\Users\ppetr\.codex\generated_images\019fedcc-f121-75e0-a2ad-d42c08ea780d\exec-352b1154-477a-4a0b-a0cf-46c0a34c6006.png`.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Today retains the authenticated shell, greeting, shared deterministic alert authority, triage facts and every current Today destination while adopting the selected warm-editorial timeline hierarchy.
2. A bounded focus timeline presents at most three authorized priorities in deterministic order. The focused item exposes reason, due context, stored evidence/source and safe primary/secondary actions; no prediction, revenue, probability or unsupported deadline is introduced.
3. The focus advances slowly only when more than one item exists, provides an explicit pause/play control, pauses on hover/focus, and becomes a stable ordered presentation under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
4. Attention balance summarizes only existing first-contact, overdue, due-today and celebration facts. Relationship moments and empty/all-clear states remain truthful and actionable.
5. Operational status is shown only when an existing authorized source provides it. Unsupported connector health is omitted or marked unavailable; no decorative “connected” claim is introduced.
6. The composition is responsive at 390 px, 768 px and desktop, with no horizontal page overflow, 44 px controls, visible focus, semantic headings/landmarks and color-independent labels.
7. The implementation reuses the current token system, Lucide dependency, Alert/Today application seams and route; it adds no schema, alert rules, provider writes, background job or new route.
8. Focused component tests, lint, typecheck, full tests, build and same-viewport browser/design QA pass. The final `design-qa.md` records comparison against the selected visual target and ends with `final result: passed`.
