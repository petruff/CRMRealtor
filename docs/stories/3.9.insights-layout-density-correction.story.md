## Story

**As a** realtor reviewing business health,  
**I want** pipeline evidence to remain legible at every supported width,  
**so that** stage counts and evidence never appear clipped, decorative or misleading.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Pipeline stage metrics render as complete bordered cards without clipped first/last cells or a decorative empty grid cell.
2. The layout reflows from one to two to three columns without horizontal overflow.
3. Empty transition history uses plain language and does not imply a measured duration or conversion rate.
4. Operational actions retain link semantics while using button affordances of at least 44px.
5. Existing stored-evidence definitions, counts and financial evidence boundary remain unchanged.
6. Lint, typecheck, focused tests and build pass; browser evidence is reported separately.
