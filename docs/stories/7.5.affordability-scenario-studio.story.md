## Story

As a realtor helping a client reason about a property, I need transparent affordability scenarios, so I can compare assumptions without presenting estimates as lender, insurer, tax, legal, or association quotes.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Scenario inputs support price, down payment, loan term/rate, property tax, homeowners/flood insurance, HOA/condo fee, assessment, maintenance, and bounded closing-cost items.
2. Every input identifies value, unit, source type/reference, as-of date, freshness, verification state, and whether it is an assumption.
3. Calculations are deterministic, reproducible, versioned, and covered by precision/property tests; unknown inputs remain unknown.
4. Monthly and cash-to-close outputs separate principal/interest, taxes, insurance, association costs, assessments, maintenance, and closing items.
5. Scenario comparison shows exact differences and assumptions without ranking a property, guaranteeing affordability, or generating a quote.
6. Optional contact/property/transaction links are workspace-scoped and do not change their canonical state.
7. Sharing or partner handoff requires consent, exact preview, approved recipient/channel, immutable intent, and receipt; no send occurs in this story.
8. Owner/assistant permissions, sensitive-data minimization, export controls, and cross-workspace isolation are enforced.
9. Desktop/mobile/PWA UX handles currency/rate inputs, long labels, error recovery, keyboard, zoom, screen reader, and reduced motion.
10. Migration, RLS, calculation, replay, privacy, accessibility, full quality, and recovery gates pass.
