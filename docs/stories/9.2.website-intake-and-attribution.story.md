# Story 9.2 — Website Lead Intake and Attribution

**Epic:** 9 — Property Intelligence and Lead Conversion
**Status:** Locally Complete — Real Website UAT Conditional
**Owner:** @architect + @data-engineer + @dev
**Quality Gate:** @qa + @devops

## Story

As a Florida realtor, I need website forms to enter Omnix with their consent, source, campaign, identity, classification, and response obligation intact, so that every valid lead is organized once and receives timely attention without exposing workspace authority to the public browser.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Public intake is bound to one server-configured workspace and endpoint; request payload cannot select a workspace, actor, or owner.
2. The exact raw request is signed with HMAC-SHA256 and a bounded timestamp; invalid, stale, malformed, or missing signatures fail before parsing or persistence.
3. Body, field, batch, origin, and per-endpoint request rate are bounded; identifiers and support outcomes never expose internal workspace, contact, membership, or submission IDs.
4. First/last name plus email or phone are normalized through the canonical contact import and identity resolver; ambiguous identities enter review and never merge destructively.
5. Exact source, medium, campaign, form, landing page, referrer, first/last touch, and conversion evidence remain append-only.
6. Channel consent captures explicit granted/declined/unknown state, policy version, capture method, and time without converting website consent into provider authorization.
7. Classification is deterministic, versioned, explainable, and limited to Hot/Warm/Nurture plus qualification state; it cannot invent intent, silently overwrite protected human decisions, or change transaction state.
8. Accepted intake creates one idempotent response obligation using the endpoint's configured SLA and responsible owner; retry cannot duplicate contact, task, attribution, consent, or receipt.
9. Processing, replay, conflict, rate limit, origin denial, ambiguous identity, downstream failure, and recovery return safe outcomes with a support reference.
10. Additive migration, rollback/forward repair, RLS/RPC tests, domain/security/application/route tests, lint, typecheck, full test, build, and negative-path evidence pass before release.

## Non-Goals

- A public workspace selector, anonymous CRM reads, autonomous outbound communication, paid lead-source scraping, or bypassing channel-specific provider consent.
- Treating an OAuth callback, form submission, or import receipt alone as proof that downstream communication is operational.
