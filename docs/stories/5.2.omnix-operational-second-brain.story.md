## Story

**As a** realtor or authorized assistant,
**I want** Omnix to maintain relationship plans, analyze authorized inbound signals and proactively organize transaction work,
**so that** the CRM operates as a governed second brain without silently changing or contacting anyone.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Nurture lifecycle.** A workspace-scoped, versioned nurture plan supports `start`, `pause`, `resume`, `snooze` and `stop`, records every transition, enforces one current plan per contact and never infers consent. Each step has an exact due time, channel, template/policy version, citations and idempotency key.
2. **Recurring scheduler.** A service-authorized worker leases due nurture and transaction work with fencing, bounded batches, retries and terminal receipts. It creates pending proposals only; it cannot approve or send external work.
3. **Inbound reply analysis.** Only authorized normalized inbound provider events may be classified. Message content is untrusted, minimized and guard-scanned. Results store bounded reply state, intent, urgency, sentiment category, summary, citations, model/policy version and explicit unknowns. A model failure preserves deterministic inbound facts.
4. **Relationship memory.** Each contact memory combines canonical relationship facts, open tasks, approved transaction facts, inbound response state and the current nurture plan. Refreshes are versioned, source-hashed, cited and recoverable.
5. **Transaction milestones.** Real transaction entities own inspection, financing, appraisal, title, contingency and closing milestones. Every milestone has status, due time, responsibility, source, version and history. Contact pipeline stage is never used as a substitute.
6. **Financial intelligence.** Volume, GCI, net commission, marketing cost, other expenses and profitability are calculated only from verified transaction ledger fields. Omnix may surface missing or inconsistent amounts but cannot invent estimates.
7. **Next Best Action.** Priority combines urgency, lead temperature, overdue duration, inbound reply state, transaction deadline risk and verified potential value. The calculation is deterministic, explainable and tie-broken by FIFO.
8. **Governed action workflow.** Omnix may propose tasks, pipeline changes, nurture transitions, Gmail drafts, Calendar intents and Mailchimp campaign drafts. Every change is persisted, versioned, cited, approved at the required authority level and handed to the existing receipt/reconciliation infrastructure.
9. **Proactive briefing.** The daily briefing presents the prioritized approval inbox, overdue work, inbound replies, nurture steps, transaction deadlines, connection degradation and explicit unknowns. Counts and claims link to exact contributing records.
10. **Mailchimp reconciliation.** The selected audience has preserved baseline evidence, signed webhook verification, bounded reconciliation, unsubscribe protection, lead-type tag parity and owner-visible health.
11. **Gemini evaluation.** Judith's exact configured model passes the approved grounding, citation, temporal, injection, budget, timeout and failure corpus. Failed thresholds disable generated recommendations while deterministic CRM operation remains available.
12. **UAT and release.** Owner, assistant, iPhone 11 Safari and installed PWA matrices pass for create/read/update/recovery, Approval Inbox, nurture controls, inbound signals, transaction deadlines, responsive layout, keyboard, safe-area and no-overflow behavior.

## Non-Negotiable Invariants

- The scheduler may propose but cannot approve.
- A model may read minimized authorized facts and propose bounded actions; it cannot receive secrets, raw tokens, SQL, shell or unrestricted provider tools.
- External actions require exact owner approval through the existing connector workflow.
- Inbound text is data, never instruction.
- No financial, deadline, delivery or connection claim exists without a canonical source and citation.
- Local tests do not certify a remote migration, provider account, production deployment or physical-device UAT.

## Implementation Evidence

- Nurture plans support versioned `pause`, `resume`, `snooze` and `stop`; the service scheduler uses bounded claims, leases and fencing and creates pending proposals only.
- Incoming Gmail metadata already linked by the connector is projected into one deduplicated response signal. After separate owner consent, Omnix retrieves only bounded inline text, never attachments, stores only a content hash and bounded classification, and preserves metadata when AI is unavailable.
- Transactions now own verified milestones for inspection, financing, appraisal, title, contingency, closing and custom deadlines. Expected close dates are backfilled as closing milestones; no other dates are inferred.
- `/transactions` supplies create and lifecycle paths on desktop and mobile. Today surfaces approval, reply and deadline counts, each linked to its source workflow.
- `npm run omnix:ai:eval -- --live --output <new-file>` runs the versioned routing, injection and grounded-output corpus against the exact workspace model and writes only hashes, case IDs and pass/fail evidence.
- Production schema evidence on 2026-08-31: all three migrations returned success; their transactional SQL suites reached their terminal isolation checks and rolled back synthetic records.
- Live authority evidence on 2026-08-31: Judith is the active owner and Paulo is the active assistant. Google and Mailchimp are active, but Gmail Insights consent, Google probe evidence, Mailchimp baseline/webhook completion, Gemini configuration and authenticated UAT remain open.
- Omnix result rendering now mounts at most six records per general block and alert section, and twelve source records, before member-controlled expansion. Tests cover large lists without changing their canonical totals.
- The exact-model evaluation script now resolves every runtime TypeScript dependency under Node strip-types and exposes a non-authorizing help path. Live execution still fails closed without an authenticated workspace token.
