# Story 7.4 — Versioned Florida Workflow Packs

**Epic:** 7 — Florida Realtor Operating System
**Status:** In Progress
**Owner:** @dev (Vulcan)
**Product/Legal Review:** required before any pack is marked current
**Quality Gate:** @qa (Argus)

## Story

As a Florida realtor, I need versioned operational workflow packs for common transaction scenarios, so I can prepare the right work while seeing the source, effective date, responsibility, and legal boundary.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Pack types cover buyer, seller, condo/co-op, flood, association, and closing scenarios through data-driven definitions rather than hard-coded pages.
2. Each pack and step stores version, effective date, source title/link, review state, responsible role, evidence requirement, acknowledgement requirement, and legal boundary.
3. Only reviewed current versions can be started; outdated, withdrawn, draft, or unknown versions remain visibly unavailable.
4. Starting a pack creates a version-bound plan and proposal-only steps; it cannot silently create verified deadlines, send communications, complete acknowledgements, or reproduce restricted forms.
5. Pack update never mutates an active plan's historical definition; migration to a later version requires explicit review and records differences.
6. Completion, skip, blocked, and acknowledgement states retain actor, time, reason, evidence reference, and history.
7. Every recommendation identifies that Omnix is an operational tool, not legal advice, and directs the realtor to authoritative brokerage/legal guidance where appropriate.
8. Owner/assistant, revoked, cross-workspace, replay, stale-version, withdrawn-pack, and missing-source paths fail closed.
9. The UX is calm, bounded, and accessible on desktop/mobile/PWA with source and legal context behind useful disclosure.
10. Schema, RLS, fixtures, source review, tests, accessibility, full gates, and recovery evidence pass.

## Implementation Evidence

- The domain defines buyer, seller, condo/co-op, flood, association, and closing pack types; draft/reviewed/withdrawn review states; version-bound definitions; sourced steps; evidence; acknowledgement; responsibility; and legal boundaries.
- A pack can be marked current only when it is reviewed and has a recorded review reference. HTTPS source links, unique bounded step keys, and legal disclosures are validated.
- Starting a plan requires a reviewed current pack and copies an immutable definition snapshot plus proposal-only steps. Later withdrawal cannot rewrite the active plan's historical definition.
- Step completion requires current version, evidence when required, acknowledgement when required, actor, time, reason, and immutable before/after snapshots.
- RLS and governed RPCs preserve workspace isolation. Direct authenticated writes to definitions, plans, steps, and events are denied.
- The transaction CLI now lists workflow packs/plans, starts plans, and transitions steps through the same repository authority used by the UI.
- Transactions presents the reviewed pack selector, responsibility, version/source/legal disclosure, bounded step controls, evidence entry, acknowledgement, and a truthful disabled state when no legally reviewed pack exists.
- Focused domain, repository, action-export, CLI, and the 16-assertion pgTAP workflow suite pass.
- Remaining before closure: product/legal review and approved pack content import, explicit version-difference migration UX, responsive/accessibility evidence, full gates after later sprint integration, and authenticated owner/assistant UAT. No pack is claimed production-current.
