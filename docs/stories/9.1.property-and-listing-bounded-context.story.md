# Story 9.1 — Property and Listing Bounded Context

**Epic:** 9 — Property Intelligence and Lead Conversion
**Status:** Locally Complete — Release Evidence Conditional
**Owner:** @architect + @data-engineer + @dev
**Quality Gate:** @qa + @ux-design-expert

## Story

As a Florida realtor, I need property identities, sourced facts, relationship interests, and transaction links to remain distinct and traceable, so that Omnix can help with property work without treating an address as a person, a lead stage, or an unlicensed market fact.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Property identity is workspace-scoped and uses a normalized address key without becoming contact identity or transaction authority.
2. Property type and lifecycle are bounded; absent facts remain unknown.
3. Every property fact records field, value, authority, source reference, as-of time, permitted-use state, optional display/retention limits, version, and actor.
4. Manual and licensed-provider facts may coexist. Licensed facts require provider identity and recorded permission; revoked, restricted, expired, or stale-display facts fail closed.
5. Contact interests support inquiry, saved, favorite, showing intent, seller-owned, and seller-prospect states with source, version, history, and explicit archive.
6. Optional transaction links are workspace-scoped and do not mutate transaction status, contact pipeline, property facts, or relationship state.
7. Create/edit/link commands enforce membership, optimistic concurrency, idempotency, cross-workspace isolation, and append-only revisions.
8. `/properties` provides bounded create, browse, sourced-fact, interest, and transaction-link paths on desktop and mobile without implying MLS/IDX activation.
9. Provider-neutral licensed data remains disabled until Story 9.3 records authorization, display, retention, deletion, freshness, and attribution rules.
10. Migration replay, rollback/forward repair, pgTAP/RLS, domain, repository, UI, responsive, accessibility, lint, typecheck, test, and build evidence pass before release.
