## Story

**As a** solo real estate agent,
**I want** to open my CRM and immediately see who needs my attention today, ordered by urgency,
**so that** I stop losing deals to contacts I forgot to follow up with.

**Traceability:** Client §8 — *"I wanted them organized by HOT to Nurture because that would tell me who needs my immediate attention and it didn't do that."* This is her #1 stated grievance and the reason the triage view is the home screen rather than a contact list.

---

## Context: applied realtor frameworks

Content and behaviour follow established real estate practice, not invented process:

| Framework | Origin | How it appears here |
|---|---|---|
| **Speed to Lead** | Industry standard (MIT/InsideSales) — odds of contact fall sharply after 5 minutes | Uncontacted new leads surface at the very top of triage, ahead of overdue follow-ups |
| **8×8** | Brian Buffini | New contacts get an 8-touch/8-week onboarding cadence before dropping into their steady rhythm |
| **33 Touch** | Brian Buffini | Sphere + past clients receive ~33 touches/year (≈ monthly), which is exactly her Nurture cadence |
| **Sphere of Influence (SOI)** | Standard practice | `relationship` axis (Lead / Active / Past Client / Sphere) modelled **separately** from `leadType` temperature — they answer different questions |
| **Homeaversary** | Standard retention practice | Purchase-date anniversary treated as a first-class recurring touchpoint alongside birthdays |
| **Smart Lists** | FollowUp Boss pattern | Triage is a set of prioritised buckets, not one flat sorted table |

**Key modelling decision:** temperature (Hot/Warm/Nurture) and relationship (Lead/Active/Past/Sphere) are orthogonal. A past client can be Hot. Collapsing them — as many simple CRMs do — is why agents lose track of their sphere.

## Cadence rules (client-confirmed 2026-08-10)

| Lead type | Interval | Triage weight |
|---|---|---|
| Hot | **7 days** | 3 |
| Warm | **7 days** | 2 |
| Nurture | **30 days** | 1 |

Hot and Warm share an interval by her choice. Weight is therefore what separates them in the sort — Hot outranks Warm at equal overdueness. Completing a touch auto-schedules the next; every date stays manually overridable.

## Acceptance Criteria

**AC1 — Contact model reflects real estate practice**
Fields: names, phone(s), email, mailing address, birthdate, home purchase date, `leadType`, `relationship`, `intent` (Buyer/Seller/Both/Investor/Renter), `source` (Cold Call / Open House / Referral / Social Media / Website / Mailer / Other — matching her actual channels), `pipelineStage`, buyer criteria (price range, areas, timeline, pre-approval), seller criteria, `referredBy`, `lastContactedAt`, `nextTouchAt`, tags.

**AC2 — Cadence engine**
Pure, unit-tested functions compute `nextTouchAt` from lead type, honour manual overrides, and apply the 8×8 accelerated cadence to contacts newer than 8 weeks.

**AC3 — Triage priority scoring**
Deterministic score from days-overdue × lead-type weight, with uncontacted new leads (Speed to Lead) ranked above everything.

**AC4 — Triage home screen**
Buckets in order: Needs First Contact → Overdue → Due Today → Birthdays & Homeaversaries (7/30-day windows) → Coming Up. Empty states are encouraging, not blank.

**AC5 — Mobile-first, premium desktop**
Built up from 375px. Single-column, thumb-reachable actions on mobile; the desktop layout earns its extra space rather than stretching. Light and dark themes both deliberate.

**AC5 design clarification — client-directed 2026-08-10**
Apply the supplied Apple marketing design contract as a product-design language, adapted to the CRM rather than copied literally:

- Preserve the `--sk-*` token namespace, SF/system font stack, optical tracking, blue primary actions, link-style secondary actions, pill controls, generous whitespace, and quiet neutral surfaces.
- Keep Hot / Warm / Nurture colors as semantic operational signals required by AC3–AC4; they are not decorative brand accents.
- Reduce repeated card chrome and build hierarchy with typography, spacing, grouped surfaces, and progressive disclosure.
- Keep every interactive target at least 44×44px where the mobile workflow is one-handed; retain visible focus, reduced-motion support, zoom, safe-area handling, and AA contrast.
- Preserve every route, field, status, bucket, integration disclosure, and honest sample-data message already required by this story.

**AC6 — Notes are first-class**
Timestamped, append-only activity log per contact. Traces to §8 — *"I loved that I could put notes in there."*

**AC7 — Runs without cloud credentials**
Typed repository interface with a seeded in-memory implementation so the UI is demonstrable before Supabase exists. Swapping implementations touches one module.
